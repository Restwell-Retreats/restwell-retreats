## What belongs on this page

Lift these straight from the printed Welcome Guide so the two never disagree:

- **Arrival.** Check-in from 3pm via the key safe. The address, what3words, satnav details, key safe code and a description of the house all go out in the confirmation email and the printed guide, **never on this page**, because the URL is reachable by anyone who guesses it.
- **WiFi.** Network and code, and the line about giving us a ring after two careful attempts rather than a third.
- **The equipment.** What’s in the house, that it’s set up around what you told us at booking, and to tell us immediately if anything feels loose or faulty.
- **Heating and comfort.** Controls within reach from a seated position. Extra blankets or a fan on request.
- **The bathroom.** Only flush toilet paper. The plumbing is retro. Wipes, sanitary products, continence products and nappies go in the bathroom bin.
- **Bins.** General waste and recycling every other Monday, food waste every Monday, calendar on the fridge, and bring them back in the same day.
- **House rules.** Dogs by prior arrangement, no smoking or vaping indoors, quiet between 10pm and 8am, five guests maximum, tell us about any damage, keep walkways clear.
- **Emergencies.** 999 first then tell us, 111 for non-urgent medical, 101 for police, 0800 111 999 for gas. Nearest A&E is QEQM in Margate. Maintenance and safety on 01622 809881.
- **Local practicalities.** Supermarkets, pharmacies, Abacus Cars on 01227 277745 for accessible taxis, the station and the 400 bus.
- **Extra services.** Mid-stay clean or fresh linen on longer bookings, flexible check-in and check-out where we can, and the portable ramps you can borrow for days out.
- **Before you head home.** Lights, heating and appliances off, windows closed, towels in the bathroom, bins out if it’s collection day, indoor liners into the outdoor bin, key back in the safe.
