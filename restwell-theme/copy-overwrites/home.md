# Home `/`

**Layer:** `hero_heading` `hero_subheading` `meta_title` `meta_description`. Never “fully accessible” in Restwell-owned copy. Guest review cards stay the reviewer’s words.

**What this site is.** Marketing for a holiday let. Its job is to make somebody want the stay and then enquire. It borrows the Welcome Guide’s *voice* (warm, plain, honest about limits) but not its *content*. Bin days, house rules, plumbing, emergency numbers and departure checklists belong in `/guest-guide/` and the welcome pack, never on a page someone reads while deciding.

**Sell first, prove second.** Specifications reassure, they don’t persuade. Lead with what the holiday is like and let the numbers back it up underneath. The full spec belongs on `/accessibility/`, where someone has gone looking for it.

**Section order (seven, down from ten):** hero → the bungalow → gallery → reviews → why this house → optional care → FAQ → enquire. Fold `paths` into `partners`, or drop `paths` and let the FAQ do the routing.

**Privacy:** no house number, street name, postcode, what3words or identifying description of the house anywhere on the public site. Whitstable and “a quiet residential street” are as specific as we go. The address and arrival details go out after a booking is confirmed, never on a page.

## The bungalow (`#property`)

Eyebrow: The bungalow

H2: One bungalow, and it’s all yours

It sits on a quiet residential street in Whitstable, about ten minutes from the seafront. The driveway is private and level, takes two cars including an adapted vehicle, and the front door is straight ahead of you when you park. We’ll send you the address and directions once your stay is confirmed.

Inside, everything is on one floor. There’s no lift to wait for, no corridor to share, and nobody else’s door to pass on your way to the kitchen at midnight. The house sleeps up to five, so there’s room for a partner, a PA, or simply a bit of quiet between you.

It’s wheelchair friendly because somebody measured. 965mm at the front, 926mm through the internal doorways, a level-access wet room with a tilt-in-space shower commode chair, and a ceiling track hoist rated to 180kg over the profiling bed. There’s a mobile hoist as well, a standing aid, and a basin that winds up and down to your height. If you need a second profiling bed, we’ll set one up. All of it is there to be used. That’s rather the point of it.

At the back, French doors open onto a level patio and the garden, with a non-slip threshold ramp taking the lip off the doorway so the BBQ isn’t a second project. We also keep a set of portable fold-up ramps you’re welcome to borrow and take out with you for the day.

## Why a house rather than a hotel

H2: A wet room in a hotel still comes with the corridor

Plenty of hotels will tell you they have an accessible room. What they don’t mention is the lift you queue for, the breakfast room you cross, and the feeling of being a logistical problem in somebody else’s building.

Here you’re on one level from the driveway to the garden, with only the people you came with. The linen and towels are already on the beds. The heating is within reach from a seated position, so you can set the temperature without a stretch or a struggle. Nobody is going to knock unless you’ve asked them to.

## Can we add home care?

H2: Yes if you want it, and no, you don’t have to

Plenty of guests bring their own person, or simply manage the way they do at home, and that’s completely fine. The bungalow rate is the same either way.

If you would like professional home care while you’re here, Continuity of Care Services can come to you. They’re our sister company, they already know this house (the wet room, the hoist, where everything is), and they’re rated Good by the CQC. That might be a morning visit to help with getting up and dressed, nurse-led support for more complex needs, or a few hours so that the people who usually do the caring can go and look at the sea instead.

One conversation covers both. Continuity invoice their care separately, and the bungalow rate stays exactly the same. Ring 01622 809881 if that’s easier than a form.
