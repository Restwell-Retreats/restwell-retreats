## Intro (`prop_hero_subtitle`)

Here’s a walk round Restwell, room by room. It’s a single-storey accessible holiday bungalow on a quiet street in Whitstable. It has two bedrooms and space for up to five people.

## Bedrooms

H2: Two bedrooms and a sofa bed, for up to five

The accessible bedroom has a ceiling track hoist that covers the whole room. We set it up with one or two profiling beds, depending on what you tell us when you book.

If you’d like the furniture arranged like your room at home, just say, and we’ll have it ready.

The second bedroom next door has a double bed. The conservatory has a double sofa bed, so up to five people can stay. The hoists, the standing aid and the profiling beds are all included in the price.

## Wet room

H2: Level-access wet room

The wet room is step-free from the door to the shower. It has grab rails, a shower stool and a tilt-in-space shower chair. There’s also a wash-and-dry toilet, and a basin that swings round and moves up and down.

It’s all fitted already, so there’s nothing to hire or set up before you arrive. You’ll find the makes, models and safe working loads on the Accessibility page.

## Living room

H2: Open-plan living room

The living room is open-plan, with a rise-and-recline armchair, a sofa with pull-out footrests and a TV with Netflix. There’s room for wheelchairs, carers and family to get past each other.

We keep the floor between the sofa and the armchair clear, so a wheelchair can turn. There’s free high-speed WiFi throughout. That matters more than people admit if you need to keep an eye on work or a hospital app.

## Kitchen

H2: Kitchen with a wheel-under worktop

The kitchen has a lowered worktop you can wheel under, and an oven whose door slides away underneath. There’s also a microwave, a fridge and a dishwasher, plus plates, pans, cutlery and the cooking basics.

The hob is gas rather than induction, which is worth knowing when you plan what to cook.

## Conservatory

H2: Conservatory with level access to the patio

The conservatory has a fold-out table to eat at and a double sofa bed. Its doors open out level onto the patio.

The laundry cupboards are in here too, with a washing machine and a tumble dryer.

## Outside

H2: Level patio, enclosed garden, and private driveway

French doors open onto a level patio, with a non-slip ramp over the threshold. The garden is fully enclosed, with outdoor seating, a barbecue and fairy lights.

Dogs are welcome. Please let us know before you arrive, so we can do a quick risk assessment.

At the front there’s a level, resin-bound driveway with space for two cars. We’ve tested it with two wheelchair-accessible vehicles. Portable ramps are kept in the outdoor box by the front door, for the house or for days out.

## Where you are

H2: On a quiet street, ten minutes from the seafront

The bungalow is on a quiet residential street, a short walk from The Plough pub. The seafront is about ten minutes away on foot.

The beach there is shingle, but the wide, paved promenade gives you a step-free route along the coast. Our Whitstable guide covers where to eat, where to park and which places have an accessible loo.
