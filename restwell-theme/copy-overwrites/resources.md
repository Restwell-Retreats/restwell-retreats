## H1 (`res_heading`)

Paying for a break, without the guesswork

## Opening paragraph (print directly under the H1)

Restwell can invoice you directly, a local authority, the NHS, or a grant body, and the bungalow costs the same whoever pays. Home care from Continuity of Care Services is invoiced separately by them. We can’t promise that your funding will cover a holiday. That decision sits with your social worker or case manager.

## Intro (`res_intro`)

Money is usually the bit that stalls a holiday, and it’s rarely because the sums don’t work. It’s because nobody will give you a straight answer about who’s allowed to pay for what.

We can’t fix that entirely. A great deal of it sits with your local authority rather than with us. But we can be completely clear about our end of it, and we can wait. Nothing is due until you’ve confirmed the house fits, so you can go and have the conversation with your funder before anybody asks you for a deposit.

If your funder calls it a respite break, that’s fine. That’s often the language they use. It’s still a private bungalow rather than a respite centre, and we’ll describe it accurately on any paperwork you need.

## A Disabled Facilities Grant won’t pay for this

H2: It sounds like it should, and it doesn’t

A Disabled Facilities Grant is for adaptations to your own home: ramps, stairlifts, a downstairs bathroom. It doesn’t cover a holiday, however much the name suggests otherwise. We mention it because people arrive here having been told it might, and it’s better to know now.

If you’re looking at direct payments for the accommodation or for a PA’s time, start here → `/direct-payment-holiday-accommodation/`

## The house and the care go on separate invoices

Restwell invoices the bungalow. Continuity of Care Services invoice any care you’ve asked for. That separation is deliberate and it usually helps, because funders frequently treat accommodation and care as two different budgets with two different people signing them off.

One enquiry starts both conversations, on the same phone number: 01622 809881. Tell us who we need to speak to and we’ll send the paperwork to the right desk.

## Grants and charities

Some guests put a grant or a charitable fund towards a break. The names change, the criteria change, and the eligibility is genuinely theirs to decide rather than ours to predict.

What we can do is invoice whichever body you name, promptly and in whatever format they need. What we won’t do is imply we can get you a yes.

## NHS Continuing Healthcare

If someone has a CHC package, the conversation is usually about whether care can continue during a stay away from home, not about the NHS paying for the cottage.

It’s worth asking your co-ordinator early, because the answer varies by area and by package. Tell us who to speak to and we’ll take part in that conversation. We won’t claim your package covers Restwell.
