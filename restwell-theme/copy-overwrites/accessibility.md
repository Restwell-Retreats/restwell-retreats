## Getting in

The driveway is resin-bound, level, and takes two cars including an adapted vehicle. There’s no step up into the house from where you park.

The porch is enclosed, with white-framed glass double doors giving a **1720mm** opening. Beyond it, the inner front door gives a **965mm clear opening**. There’s a full-width ribbed entrance mat across the porch floor.

Internal doorways throughout are **926mm clear**. The bedroom and wet room doors are white panelled with black lever handles and black latch plates. The contrast is deliberate, so the doors are easier to pick out if you have low vision or dementia.

We also keep a set of portable fold-up ramps for the front door. They’re yours while you’re here, and you’re welcome to take them out for the day.

## The wet room

Level-access throughout, with no lip at the entrance.

**Shower.** A Mira Select Flex thermostatic mixer with a separate temperature dial and flow lever, on a slide rail. It’s TMV3-approved, so the maximum temperature is capped, and it carries RNIB Tried & Tested accreditation for its high-contrast markings.

**Shower commode chair.** A RAZ-AT tilt-in-space rehab shower commode chair: tilts to 40°, backrest reclines to 90°, seat height 560mm, footplates adjustable between 410mm and 520mm, flip-up arms, swing-away footrests, padded headrest, and a Kinetic Balance hip belt. Rear castors are braked. It has a commode pan and cover tray.

**Shower stool.** A separate Drive DeVilbiss free-standing bath and shower stool, height-adjustable from 340mm to 520mm, rated to **136kg**.

**Basin.** A Ropox Swing height-adjustable washbasin on a Dock-In unit, adjustable between **750mm and 950mm** by manual crank, with a thermostatic mixer tap.

**WC.** A Geberit AquaClean Mera Care shower toilet: warm-water wash and dryer, operated by a large remote with a rotary dial.

**Support rail.** A NYMAS hinged lift-and-lock toilet support rail with a drop-down leg. It must be lifted to unlock before lowering, and only used as support when horizontal.

**Emergency alarm.** A pull-cord assistance alarm with a reset plate and an over-door light.

There’s a bin in the wet room for continence products, and it’s emptied between every stay.

## The accessible bedroom

**Ceiling track hoist.** An Amico GoLift 400, ceiling-mounted, with a **safe working load of 180kg**. The carry bar itself is rated higher, but the motor governs the system and that’s the figure that counts. It’s subject to a LOLER thorough examination every six months.

**Mobile hoist.** There’s also an Oxford Midi 180 electric mobile hoist, **rated to 180kg**: maximum height 1840mm, minimum 1240mm, turning radius 1235mm, legs opening to 1170mm externally and closing to 600mm. Also under six-monthly LOLER examination.

**Profiling bed.** An Accora CommunityBed, **maximum user weight 180kg**, lifting range 220mm to 800mm, with backrest auto-regression and a kneebreak. The mattress platform is 900 × 2000mm and the overall footprint is 930 × 2250mm. It was manufactured new in November 2025.

**A second profiling bed is available.** We set the room up around the guest: if one bed is what you need, that’s what’s in there; if you need two, we set both up. Tell us when you book.

**Mattress.** Static pressure-management. Depending on the bed, either an Accora Allevia Comfort FirmEdge, dual-layer foam with a reinforced perimeter for transfer stability, rated to 160kg, or a Roma Medical MATT 1 reflex foam mattress with a waterproof, vapour-permeable cover.

**Standing aid.** An AAL RS4 non-powered sit-to-stand transfer aid, **rated to 185kg**, with a knee pad, reinforced grab bar, footplate and dual rear-locking brakes. Seat height adjusts between 620mm and 670mm. It suits someone who can weight-bear and rise actively.

**Slings.** Slings are prescribed for each person and fitted to them and to the hoist, so we can’t safely supply them. Both hoists here take loop-style slings on a two-point spreader bar, so please bring the sling the person already uses.

**Bringing your own hoist.** The bed lowers to 220mm. The Oxford Midi’s legs stand 100mm high with 25mm of ground clearance, and it turns in 1235mm. Check your own hoist against those figures, and tell us you’re bringing it so we can set the room up.

## The kitchen, living space and garden

The kitchen is fully equipped with worktops you can reach. The oven is a NEFF Slide & Hide with a door that tucks away underneath, so you’re not reaching across a hot open door from a seated position. There’s a microwave as well.

The living room has a rise-and-recline chair. There’s free high-speed WiFi throughout, and the heating controls are within reach from a seated position. Turn it up or down yourself, and ask if you’d like extra blankets or a fan.

The conservatory has French doors giving a **1720mm** opening, garden views, a radiator, and a double sofa bed that makes the fifth sleeping space.

Rear French doors out to the patio also give **1720mm**, with a non-slip threshold ramp **20mm high and 80mm wide** removing the raised lip. That takes you onto a level patio, then the garden and BBQ area.

## Getting around Whitstable

H2: Getting around Whitstable

Whitstable is an old seaside town, so some parts are easier to get around than others. Here’s what we’ve found.

- **The good:** Tankerton promenade is a long, level, surfaced path along the seafront, and it’s the route most of our guests use. There’s free Blue Badge parking along Marine Parade. There are accessible toilets along the promenade, and a Changing Places toilet at the harbour. The streets around the bungalow are level and paved, with dropped kerbs.
- **The challenges:** Harbour Street and the old town have narrow pavements that get crowded at weekends and in summer. Some shops and cafes have a step at the door and no ramp. The ground around the harbour and the fish market can be uneven. Weekday mornings are the easiest time to visit.

Our Whitstable guide lists the routes, cafes and accessible toilets we know work. We send the same notes in your welcome pack. “Whitstable guide” → `/whitstable-area-guide/`
