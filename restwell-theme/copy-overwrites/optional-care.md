## Intro (`care_intro`)

Our sister company, Continuity of Care Services, can come into the bungalow while you’re here, but only if you ask. How much help they can give depends on what you need and who’s free, so we’ll talk it through with you first.

## What they can actually do

H2: You choose how much, and it isn’t a package

It might be a simple morning visit to help with getting up and getting dressed. It might be nurse-led support for more complex needs: hoisting, PEG feeding, that level of thing. Or it might be a few hours here and there so that the people who normally do the caring can walk down to Tankerton and look at the water without watching the clock.

You tell us what a good day would look like, and we work backwards from there. There’s no minimum package to squeeze yourself into.

Because the equipment is already fitted here, Continuity aren’t improvising around a standard holiday let. The hoist is over the bed. The chair is in the wet room. That is a genuinely different starting point from bringing carers into an ordinary rental.

## How soon can it be arranged?

H2: Sooner if you ask sooner, and we won’t pretend otherwise

We don’t publish a lead time, because we’d only be making it up. It depends on what you need and who’s available that week. What we will do is give you a straight answer quickly, rather than a maybe that drifts.

Say what you’re thinking when you first write to us, even if your dates are vague. It costs you nothing and it gives us the best chance of saying yes.

## Who holds the CQC rating

Continuity of Care Services are rated Good by the Care Quality Commission, at location 1-2624556588. That registration is theirs. Victoria Walker, who owns Restwell, is Continuity’s registered manager.

Restwell isn’t a registered care provider and doesn’t pretend to be one. We’re a house. We mention the rating so that you know exactly who is accountable for the care, and can go and read the report yourself.
