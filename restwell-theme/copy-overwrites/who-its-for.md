## Intro (`wif_intro`)

Restwell is a private holiday home for disabled adults, their carers, families and friends. Wheelchair users, people with learning disabilities, and guests with or without a PA are all welcome. It sleeps five, and it isn’t a care home or respite centre.

## If you’re coming as a guest or a family

You want to know whether you’ll get through the door, and whether you’ll be able to shower without an ordeal. So: the front door is 965mm and the internal doorways are 926mm, the wet room is level-access, and there’s a ceiling track hoist over the profiling bed. Somebody measured all of it.

The whole house is yours, which changes the feel of a week more than people expect. No corridor. No lift. No other guests deciding when it’s quiet. You get up when you get up.

The full numbers are on `/accessibility/`.

## If you’re the one doing the caring

You want a holiday too, and that’s not a selfish thing to want.

There’s room here to help someone without living on top of them: five beds across two bedrooms and a conservatory sofa bed, so you’re not sharing a room with the person you spend all day supporting. The kit is fitted, so you’re not lifting in ways you shouldn’t.

And if school holidays are simply impossible in your household, a quiet midweek stay out of season is often the easier week to actually enjoy. That’s not a promise about what’s free. Just an observation about what this house is good at.

If you’d like some of the caring taken off you while you’re here, Continuity of Care Services can do that → `/optional-care/`

## If you’re an occupational therapist or case manager

Everything we know about the house is published, including the things that aren’t ideal. Makes, models and safe working loads are all on the access statement. The harbour beach is shingle. We don’t invent turning circles.

The accessible bedroom is arranged per guest: one profiling bed or two, depending on what you tell us.

If you need a measurement that isn’t on the access statement, ring us and we’ll go and take it. We’d far rather measure something twice than have a placement fail on the first evening.

## If you’re commissioning or funding a stay

This is a private holiday let, not a local authority short-break service. Restwell provides the accommodation; Continuity of Care Services provide any care and hold the CQC registration.

The house rate doesn’t change with the funder. Only the name on the invoice does. We can bill you, a council, the NHS or a grant body. → `/resources/`

## Bringing a dog

We love dogs, and they’re welcome. Just tell us in advance so we can run a quick risk assessment and confirm it works alongside everything else in the house. Assistance dogs are welcome on exactly the same terms.
