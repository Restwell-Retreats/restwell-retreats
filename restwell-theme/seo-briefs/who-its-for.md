# SEO Content Brief — Who It's For

Source page: `restwell-theme/mockups/who-its-for-concept.html`

## 1. Keyword Strategy

SEO recommendations: this page's audience is split four ways (guests/families, carers, OTs, commissioners) — the keyword strategy should stay broad enough to serve all four without reading as generic. The two FAQ columns already do strong long-tail work around "respite vs accessible holiday" comparisons; keep that language, it's differentiating content competitors won't have.

- **Primary keyword:** "Accessible stay suitability" *(existing focus keyphrase — `inc/seo-content-seed-meta.php`, slug `who-its-for`)*
- **Secondary keywords:** "wheelchair-friendly accommodation Kent", "accessible respite holiday Whitstable", "disabled-friendly bungalow for carers", "accessible holiday for complex needs"

The current H1 ("Is Restwell right for your group?") is conversational and on-brand but doesn't carry the primary phrase — see the suggested tightened version below. The FAQ section already earns its keep on "respite care vs accessible holiday" long-tail queries; don't rewrite those answers, they're doing real comparison-content work.

## 2. Content Structure & Enhancements

- **Situations section (four personas):** good structure, keep the icon+heading+body pattern. Lightly work "accessible stay" language into the guests/families card opening line.
- **Access section:** short and links out to the full Accessibility page rather than duplicating it — correct pattern, avoids content cannibalisation between these two pages.
- **Pull-quote:** single testimonial, no heading visible (uses `sr-only` H2) — appropriate, this is a trust beat not a content section.
- **Optional care band:** dark band-teal treatment differentiates it visually from the rest of the page — good use of the "care can be added, never bundled" message that recurs sitewide; consistent phrasing across pages (property, who-its-for, care) reinforces this as a trust signal, not duplicate content, since it's contextualised differently each time.
- **Funding section:** intentionally minimal — one paragraph + link to the Funding & Support hub. Correct: this avoids duplicating Resources page content here.
- **Next steps (3-step process):** mirrors the How It Works page's process pattern at a page-appropriate scale — good internal consistency.
- **FAQ (9 questions, two columns):** genuinely strong differentiated content — "is a holiday cottage better than a respite care placement," "can we take a disabled holiday instead of care-home respite." This is the kind of comparison content that ranks and gets cited in AI answers precisely because most competitor accessible-let sites don't address it. Keep as-is.

## 3. Technical SEO

- **Meta title (existing, in sync):** `Accessible Stay Suitability | Who It Fits | Restwell` — 53 characters.
- **Meta description (existing, in sync):** `Accessible stay suitability for guests, carers, OTs and commissioners. Check who Restwell suits before you enquire about dates.` — 130 characters.
- **Headings:** single H1, clean H2 ladder across six sections (Situations → Access → Care → Funding → Next steps → FAQ), one `sr-only` H2 on the testimonial (correct pattern — keeps heading hierarchy intact without a visible redundant "Testimonial" label).
- **Image alt text:** descriptive throughout (e.g. "Amico ceiling track hoist over the bed", "Rise and recline chair providing extra support during a stay") — consistent with sitewide standard.
- **URL/slug:** live slug is `/who-its-for/`.

## 4. User Experience & Conversion

- Subnav with six in-page anchors (Situations/Access/Care/Funding/Next steps/FAQ) — good for a page serving four different reader types who may only want one section.
- Two CTA points into the Optional care band (`Learn about optional care`, `Ask about care options`) plus a funding hub link and a final mid-CTA — reasonable CTA density for a longer decision-support page.
- `data-reveal` scroll animation on three media panels — consistent with sitewide restraint (used sparingly, not on every image).

## 5. Content Length

Comfortably over 1,200 words once both FAQ columns are counted — appropriate for a page doing pre-qualification work across four distinct audiences. No padding needed.
