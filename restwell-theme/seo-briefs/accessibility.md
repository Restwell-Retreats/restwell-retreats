# SEO Content Brief — Accessibility (Access Statement)

Source page: `restwell-theme/mockups/accessibility-concept.html`

## 1. Keyword Strategy

SEO recommendations: this page's FAQ section already does excellent long-tail keyword work (10 questions targeting phrases like "ceiling hoist holiday cottage England", "profiling bed holiday cottage UK", "wheelchair friendly holiday cottage") — don't disturb that, it's a strong AEO asset as-is. The gap is earlier in the page: the H1, stat row, and room-by-room cards currently favour plain descriptive language over the actual target phrase.

- **Primary keyword:** "Wheelchair accessible holiday cottage" *(existing focus keyphrase — `inc/seo-content-seed-meta.php`, slug `accessibility`)*
- **Secondary keywords:** "accessible bungalow Whitstable", "ceiling hoist holiday cottage", "step-free holiday accommodation Kent", "wet room holiday let"

Work the primary phrase into the H1 or lede (currently "Wheelchair access details for the Restwell bungalow" — close, but doesn't contain the exact phrase "wheelchair accessible holiday cottage"). Leave the FAQ copy untouched; it already covers this ground with more precision than a rewrite would add.

## 2. Content Structure & Enhancements

- **Hero:** rework the opening sentence to land the primary phrase early — this is the single highest-impact change on the page.
- **Stat row (965mm / 926mm / full-room hoist):** genuinely strong AEO content — exact figures are what get pulled into AI-generated answers and featured snippets. Don't dilute with adjectives; keep it numeric.
- **Room-by-room cards:** good specificity already. Only the "Arrival & entrance" and "Bedrooms & sleeping" cards need a light touch to include "step-free" / "wheelchair accessible" language naturally in the opening clause.
- **Local coast comparison list:** honest about limitations (shingle beach, narrow Harbour Street pavements) — keep this. Trustworthy content like this is what separates a real access statement from marketing copy, and it's exactly the kind of nuance AI assistants tend to surface when answering "is X actually accessible."
- **FAQ section:** no changes recommended. Already covers "hospital bed holiday cottage," "profiling bed UK," "wheelchair friendly holiday cottage" definitions, and pre-booking checklists — this is doing more AEO work than most competitor sites will have.

## 3. Technical SEO

- **Meta title (existing, in sync):** `Wheelchair Accessible Holiday Cottage | Restwell Retreats` — 58 characters.
- **Meta description (existing, in sync):** `Wheelchair accessible holiday cottage access statement: ceiling hoist, level-access wet room, door widths 965mm and 926mm, plus parking.` — 141 characters, numeric specificity in the description itself is a plus.
- **Headings:** single H1, clean H2 ladder (Room-by-room → Equipment → Local coast → Access statement → Access FAQ). No skips.
- **Image alt text:** consistently descriptive across all 12 images on this page (room photos + equipment gallery) — no changes needed, this page is the model for the rest of the site.
- **URL/slug:** live slug is `/accessibility/`.

## 4. User Experience & Conversion

- Two dedicated "Specific requirement?" / "Need precise measurements?" cards funnel uncertain visitors straight to Enquire — good conversion pattern, keep.
- FAQ accordion + gallery lightbox both verified working correctly during the responsive audit (open/close, keyboard, no overflow).
- Mid-CTA at the close offers both "Enquire Now" and "Tour the property" — good secondary path for visitors not ready to enquire yet.

## 5. Content Length

This page is long (12 images, 6 spec cards, 10 FAQ items) and comfortably exceeds 1,200 words once the FAQ is counted — that's appropriate here since it's the page most likely to be the deciding factor for a booking. Don't trim the FAQ to hit a shorter target; length is doing real work on this specific page.
