# SEO Content Brief — Funding & Support (Resources)

Source page: `restwell-theme/mockups/resources-concept.html`

## 1. Keyword Strategy

SEO recommendations: this page's 11-question FAQ already covers an unusually specific and valuable long-tail cluster around NHS CHC and direct-payment funding for holidays — content most competitors won't have because it requires genuine domain knowledge, not just travel copywriting. Preserve the caveated, "check in writing before you pay a deposit" tone; it's accurate and it's also what differentiates this from a generic "funding options" page.

- **Primary keyword:** "Fund accessible respite holiday" *(existing focus keyphrase — `inc/seo-content-seed-meta.php`, slug `resources`)*
- **Secondary keywords:** "NHS CHC funded holiday accommodation", "direct payments holiday accommodation", "personal budget short break Care Act", "carers grants accessible holiday"

## 2. Content Structure & Enhancements

- **Three funding-route cards (Local authority / NHS-CHC / Grants & private):** each has a clear "Next steps" checklist with a real phone number (KCC: 03000 41 61 61) — genuinely actionable content, keep as-is.
- **Grants & key contacts directory:** links to Carers Trust, Turn2us, Respite Association, Age UK Herne Bay & Whitstable, plus direct KCC/ICB contact details — strong external-linking practice to authoritative advice sites; this kind of curated directory is exactly what earns organic backlinks from carer forums and local authority resource pages.
- **"What we can send" band:** lists concrete deliverables (written quote, access statement, invoice setup) rather than vague "we can help" language — good, specific, keep.
- **Funding FAQ (11 questions):** this is the page's strongest asset. Questions like "can I use my direct payments to pay for a holiday in England?" and "what if my funding application is refused?" (with the actual ombudsman appeals path) go well beyond what a typical accessible-accommodation site publishes. No changes recommended — this is differentiated, expert-level content that should be protected from generic rewrites.

## 3. Technical SEO

- **Meta title (existing, in sync):** `Funding an Accessible Respite Holiday | Restwell` — 48 characters.
- **Meta description (existing, in sync):** `How families fund an accessible respite break: direct payments, personal budgets under the Care Act, and NHS Continuing Healthcare. A plain-English guide.` — 154 characters.
- **Headings:** single H1, clean H2 ladder (Routes → Grants & contacts → Paperwork → Funding FAQ). Card headings inside the Routes section use H3 correctly nested under the Routes H2.
- **`id` anchors on route cards** (`#local-authority`, `#nhs`, `#private`) — good practice, allows other pages (e.g. Who It's For's funding section) to deep-link to a specific route rather than just the page top.
- **URL/slug:** live slug is `/resources/`.

## 4. User Experience & Conversion

- Subnav with four anchors (Routes/Grants & contacts/Paperwork/FAQ) — appropriate for a long, reference-style page.
- `data-reveal` scroll animation on the route cards and the closing image — consistent, restrained use matching sitewide convention.
- Closing "What we can send" band gives a concrete, low-commitment next action (request a written quote/access pack) before the harder "enquire now" ask — good funnel sequencing for a page whose readers are often still at the research stage.

## 5. Content Length

Comfortably over 1,200 words once the 11-question FAQ is included — appropriate given this is effectively the site's funding reference page. Length here is a genuine asset (depth signals expertise on a topic search engines treat cautiously — YMYL-adjacent financial/care guidance).
