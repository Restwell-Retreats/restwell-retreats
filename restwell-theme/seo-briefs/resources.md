# SEO Content Brief — Funding & Support (Resources)

Source page: `restwell-theme/mockups/resources-concept.html`

## 1. Keyword Strategy

The page owns the “how do we pay for this” query cluster. The H1 keeps the existing focus keyphrase. Long-tail CHC and direct-payment questions now sit in both the route writeups (where people actually read them) and a six-question FAQ (where they earn FAQPage rich results). Duplicate FAQ variants were merged so the page reads as a guide, not a keyword list.

- **Primary keyword:** "Fund accessible respite holiday" *(existing focus keyphrase — `inc/seo-content-seed-meta.php`, slug `resources`)*
- **Secondary keywords:** "NHS CHC funded holiday accommodation", "direct payments holiday accommodation", "personal budget short break Care Act", "carers grants accessible holiday"

## 2. Content Structure & Enhancements

- **Opening facts:** same published rate / nominated funder / two bills. The lodging-vs-care split is the page’s actual job; it is no longer buried in FAQ.
- **Three funding-route cards:** green “usually covered” vs red “usually not”, with a source line. Deep-link IDs `#local-authority`, `#nhs`, `#private` are unchanged. Copy is researched from the Care Act, the GOV.UK CHC leaflet, and named grant bodies. Nothing is promised.
- **Kent vs elsewhere:** Kent numbers are listed because Restwell is in Whitstable. Copy tells out-of-county readers to start with their own adult social care team; Restwell can still invoice them.
- **Grants & key contacts:** Carers Trust, Turn2us, Respite Association, Age UK Herne Bay & Whitstable, plus KCC, Kent & Medway ICB, and Continuity’s enquiry line.
- **Paperwork panel:** written quote, access statement, invoice setup — then enquire. No decorative bungalow photo.
- **Funding FAQ (6 questions):** distinct intents only. Overlapping CHC/direct-payment variants were folded into the route copy. Caveated tone kept: check in writing before a deposit; Restwell supplies evidence and does not award funding.

## 3. Technical SEO

- **Meta title (existing, in sync):** `Funding an Accessible Respite Holiday | Restwell` — 48 characters.
- **Meta description (existing, in sync):** `How families fund an accessible respite break: direct payments, personal budgets under the Care Act, and NHS Continuing Healthcare. A plain-English UK guide.` — 155 characters.
- **Headings:** single H1; H2 ladder (How it works → Routes → Grants → Paperwork → Funding FAQ). Route titles are H3 under Routes.
- **`id` anchors** on route articles (`#local-authority`, `#nhs`, `#private`) for deep links from Who It’s For and pricing.
- **FAQPage JSON-LD** matches the six visible questions (`restwell_output_jsonld_resources_faq()`). CollectionPage remains for the hub.
- **URL/slug:** live slug is `/resources/`.

## 4. User Experience & Conversion

- Subnav: How it works · Routes · Grants · Paperwork · FAQ.
- Next-steps panels give a phone number or jump link on every route.
- Paperwork panel is the low-commitment ask (quote and access pack). Mid-CTA at the end is the enquire.

## 5. Content Length

Shorter than the previous essay version. Depth sits in the cover/not-cover lists and source links, not in repeated paragraphs.
