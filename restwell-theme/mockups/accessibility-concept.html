Room by room

Room-by-room access notes

  • Arrival & entrance

    Private driveway for two cars, step-free path, 965mm clear front opening, level threshold. We keep portable fold-up ramps for the front if you need them.

  • Inside the property

    Single storey throughout. Internal doorways measure 926mm clear. Hall routes stay clear for chair turning.

  • Bedrooms & sleeping

    Profiling beds with a pressure-relieving mattress in the hoist-equipped accessible bedroom, plus a second double bedroom next door. Conservatory sofa bed sleeps a fifth guest.

  • Wet room

    Level-access shower, grab rails, shower chair, height-adjustable basin, wash-dry WC. Care Spaces adapted this room.

  • Kitchen

    Reachable and wheel-under worksurfaces, stocked basics, gas hob (not induction); tell us if that affects your plans.

  • Outdoor spaces

    A threshold ramp leads to a level patio and enclosed garden, with BBQ space and French doors from the conservatory.

    Email what you need early. We can’t promise every aid on short notice, but we’d rather hear from you than have you worry.

Local coast

What to expect in Whitstable

Level coast
Tankerton promenade is flat and paved for miles, with Marine Parade parking and harbour accessible WCs nearby.
Town limits
Harbour Street pavements can be narrow; some shop entrances are stepped; harbour surfaces can be uneven.
Beach
The shingle itself is not wheelchair-friendly. Use the promenade above for sea air.
