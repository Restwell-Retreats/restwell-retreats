How it works

Enquire, confirm, deposit, arrive

Four steps from first message to key-safe arrival. No online checkout. You only pay a deposit once dates are agreed.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Tell us your travel dates, who’s coming, and what equipment you need.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    We’ll set up the house and equipment for your group. If you want, we can also start a Continuity care conversation using the same number.

  3. 03

    Deposit

    Pay a 50% deposit to reserve your bungalow. The rest is due one week before you arrive.

  4. 04

    Arrive

    Arrive any time after 3pm and use the key-safe. The step-free house and all your equipment will be ready for your group.

Arrival day

Key-safe from 3pm

The step-free bungalow and access kit will be ready when you arrive. You can park on the driveway. We’ll send guest notes after your dates are confirmed.
