The Bungalow

The details, before you book

It’s not easy to find holiday homes with proper wheelchair accessibility and a ceiling-track hoist within the UK, but many guests tell us this one is worth the journey, no matter where they’re coming from.

Restwell is a private, accessible holiday bungalow on Russell Drive in Whitstable, just an hour from London. When you arrive, you’ll find a ceiling-track hoist in the bedroom, one or two profiling beds set up as you need, a wet room, step-free entrance, and off-street parking. You can check the measurements online or ask us, and we’ll explain everything.

  • Ceiling track hoist in the bedroom
  • Level-access wet room
  • Step-free entry and driveway parking

If you need support while you stay

Optional care during your stay

You book your stay through Restwell, and we can also offer home support during your visit. You can arrange this in the same phone call with our CQC-rated Good sister company, Continuity of Care Services. Call us to discuss your needs, and we’ll set it up with Continuity.

Care can include:

  • Personal care Including washing, dressing, and daily routines.
  • Visiting care Short visiting care during the day.
  • Mobility and trips Support with mobility, hoisting, or local trips.

Complex care packages need an assessment first. We won’t add anything to your stay until you’re fully happy with the plan.
