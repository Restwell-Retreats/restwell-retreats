If you need support while you stay

Optional care during your stay

You book your stay through Restwell. If you also need care while you’re here, we’ll discuss that with you by phone and arrange agreed support separately through Continuity of Care Services.

Care can include:

  • Personal care Including washing, dressing, and daily routines.
  • Visiting care Short visiting care during the day.
  • Mobility and trips Support with mobility, hoisting, or local trips.

Complex care packages need an assessment first. We won’t add anything to your stay until you’re 100% happy with the plan.
