Whole bungalow for your party. Step-free, with the access kit already in place.

Bedrooms

Ceiling hoist and profiling beds

Full-room ceiling track in the accessible bedroom. Second double bedroom next door. Conservatory sofa bed for a fifth guest. Mobile hoist and Sara Stedy available.

Door widths and kit notes

Wet room

Roll-in shower, adjustable basin

Step-free shower, grab rails, shower and commode chairs, tilt-in-space chair, height-adjustable basin, and Geberit AquaClean wash-dry WC.

Living & kitchen

Wheel-under kitchen, open plan

Rise-and-recline chair, dining space, and a lowered wheel-under worksurface. Conservatory links laundry and the patio on the level.

Outside

Patio, garden, drive for two

French doors onto a level patio via a non-slip threshold ramp. Enclosed lawn, BBQ, driveway parking for two. Portable ramps available.
