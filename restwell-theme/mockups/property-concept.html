Inside the accessible bungalow in Whitstable

A room-by-room look at Restwell: the bedrooms, wet room, living spaces, kitchen, garden and access equipment already in place.

Bedrooms

Ceiling hoist and adjustable profiling beds

The accessible bedroom is set up for comfortable transfers and restful nights, with a full-room ceiling track hoist and one or two adjustable profiling beds.

A second double bedroom sits next door, and the conservatory has a double sofa bed for occasional overnight stays. A mobile hoist and stand-aid, often known as a Sara Stedy, are also available.

Door widths and equipment notes

Wet room

Roll-in shower and accessible washroom

The step-free wet room includes a roll-in shower, grab rails, shower and commode chairs, a tilt-in-space chair, a height-adjustable 180° spin wash basin, and a Geberit AquaClean wash-dry WC.

It’s designed to make washing, toileting, and transfers more manageable during your stay.

Living room

A comfortable place to come back to

After time by the sea, the living room gives everyone space to settle in.

There’s a rise-and-recline armchair, a sofa with pull-out footrests, and a TV with Netflix. The open-plan layout helps wheelchair users, family members, and carers move around without the room feeling crowded.

Kitchen

Wheel-under kitchen, ready for everyday meals

The kitchen includes a lowered wheel-under worksurface, slide-under oven, microwave, fridge, dishwasher, plates, cutlery, utensils, and cooking basics.

It’s practical for simple breakfasts, family meals, and relaxed evenings in.

Conservatory

Sunny dining space with level garden access

The conservatory is a bright extra room for eating, reading, or looking out onto the garden.

It has a fold-out dining table, a double sofa bed, level access to the patio, and laundry cupboards with a washing machine and tumble dryer.

Outside

Level patio, enclosed garden, and private driveway

French doors open onto a level patio through a non-slip threshold ramp.

The fully enclosed, dog-friendly garden has outdoor seating, a BBQ, and fairy lights, so you can eat outside or let the dog out safely.

At the front, the resin-bound level-access driveway has parking for two cars and has been tested with two wheelchair accessible vehicles. Portable ramps are stored in the outdoor box beside the front door, ready to use around the property or take out during your stay.

Optional care

Care support, arranged separately

Professional care support is available on site through Continuity, our CQC-regulated sister company.

Care is optional, arranged separately, and never bundled into the bungalow rate. If you’d like support during your stay, we can explain how it works before you book.

Location

A quiet Whitstable base near the coast path

The bungalow sits on a quiet street, a short walk from The Plough pub and around 10–15 minutes from Tankerton promenade.

The beach is shingle, but the wide, paved promenade offers a step-free route along the coast and forms part of the King Charles III England Coast Path.

For practical route notes, local recommendations, and access information, read our Whitstable accessibility guide.
