## M.H. — Facebook Review

I was looking for an accessible holiday home with a washing toilet and a carer help for elderly, disabled Mum. Mum hasn't been away for over 15 years and I thought it would be difficult to find somewhere that could accommodate her needs.

Keelie was tremendously helpful in explaining all the facilities, equipment and care help they could provide. The fact that they could move all the furniture around to a layout suitable for Mum was fantastic. I suggested a bedroom plan for Mum - it would be reassuring for Mum to have a similar layout to her own bedroom. A couple days before we arrived Keelie video-called, and showed me around the bungalow and introduced me to Victoria and Nikki, the carers.

As the bungalow could accommodate up to 5, I invited my friend and her blind, disabled husband Baku, to join us for the weekend. They shared the double bedroom. The bungalow was brilliant for Baku's needs also.

Victoria visited each morning to wash and dress Mum. Victoria explained that we were one of the first guests to stay at the bungalow and the first to request the care service. She is the owner of the bungalow and is a care provider (she has a team working with her but wanted to serve Mum, her first client, in person). It was her concept to be able to offer an accessible holiday home with option of care service. Although she has had much support from friends, family and community, I can see that it has been her 'can do' attitude that has realised this genius project.

We were there during a heatwave and the suntrap conservatory got very warm - however, I can imagine it would be very welcomely cosy the rest of the year.

The garden was so lovely - it was accessible, private, peaceful to sit and relax with local birdlife to keep us entertained.

The bungalow is modern and spotless. It is fully equipped - for both the person you are caring for, and for the carer. The kitchen, bathroom, cleaning equipment & materials, garden furniture, bedding, towels, hairdryer, tv, fans, useful info, etc. It was a home from home.

Check in was easy and Keelie or Victoria were available for help or queries.

Whitstable is a lovley town - the Harbour Market was buzzing and friendly with access to the promenade (a bit up and down in places with wheelchair).

Sunset at Hampton Pier was stunning. (Food at Hampton Inn was excellent)

On last day, we walked 15 mins from the bungalow to the local beach - Victoria explained the best route to accessible promenade.

Thank you, Victoria!

**Suggested homepage excerpt:** Keelie was tremendously helpful in explaining all the facilities, equipment and care help they could provide. The bungalow is modern and spotless — fully equipped for both the person you are caring for, and for the carer. It was a home from home.

## M.P. — Facebook Review

I've watched, waited, patiently, while this whole project was being put together, I was so excited to see the finished article.. from the minute I turned up, rolled my wheelchair out the car, I looked up, and smiled.. from the immaculate driveway, gently sloping up to the front door, or even through the back gate, into the garden, all the way to the conservatory.. it was beautifully landscaped and designed, most importantly though, it's uniformally flat, brilliant idea.. getting into the house it was immediately obvious the hallways had been widened, great touch, the amount of times I knock into walls as there is just no turning circle, not here.. 2 really good size bedrooms.. ceiling track hoist installed too, another great touch, making it indeed accessible for ALL.. a manual hoist was available also, helped me on and off the sofa with ease.. the wet room should be in a gallery.. no expense spared in there, but for the GUEST benefit.. from the movable sink, something I have never seen before, a really fancy toilet, but again, for the guests benefit, it could assist with disposing, cleaning, drying, very clever.. then into a lovely "inclusive" open plan lounge kitchen, with stunning cooker, it's door opens and slides away, so I actually cooked my own dinner, without risk of burning myself on the oven door.. the entire house though, mirrors one philosophy, be flat, be accessible.. bar a couple of door thresholds, the whole house, is all on one level.. the same level, amazing, really well thought out, hard flooring throughout too.. and the house is still going.. from the lounge you step into one humongous conservatory and utility room.. so nice to lounge out in, then back to the immaculate garden, table & chairs, BBQ, and some fairy lights.. so peaceful too.. it truly amazes me, just how much work has gone into this "home from home" literally everything you may require, is provided.. I will most definitely be back..

## M.W. — Facebook Review

The house was well equipped with all the facilities we needed for my Dad's complex needs. Vicky and Keeley could not do enough for us, we forgot flannels and they traveled to bring us some which was very kind. We had a BBQ on the first night (cole provided) in the garden which is a lovely sun trap. Overall our stay was comfortable and a home away from home. Recommended 5 Stars.
