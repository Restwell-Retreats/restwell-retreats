- Demand we can measure: cottage, wet room, funding.
- USP: optional home care from Continuity of Care Services, our sister company, on this bungalow stay. Home snippet earns the click. `/optional-care/` owns the page. One enquiry, same phone 01622 809881.
- CQC Good is Continuity location 1-2624556588. Never Restwell. Continuity’s site is Maidstone home care. Restwell is not their holiday listing.
- Refuse: `holidays with carers provided`, `paying for care on holiday`, `respite holiday by the sea`, `disabled holidays uk`. Do not retitle Home as an operator.
- Do not advertise care lead times. Discuss what they need and work it out.
- Occupancy [NEEDS FACT]. iCal coming. No leftover nights, last-minute stock, Christmas spaces, or seasonal “from £”.
- Respite allowed. Fully accessible only in guest quotes. Wheelchair friendly only with millimetres nearby.
- One primary per URL. Five keep-guides indexable. Other mill posts noindex.
- Human titles 40–60 characters. Query language first, then the differentiator. H1 is a different sentence. Meta is proof plus enquire. Do not claim we rank.
