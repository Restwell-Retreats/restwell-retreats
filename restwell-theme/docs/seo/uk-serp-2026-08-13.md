**Both are live UK language.** “Wheelchair friendly” is not only operator copy.

- Organic titles more often say **wheelchair accessible** (Parkdean URL still `/wheelchair-friendly`). Related also offered `Wheelchair-accessible beach holidays` and `Wheelchair adapted holidays uk`.

Do not pick a winner from this pass. Copy rule still stands: wheelchair friendly is allowed next to millimetres/kit.
