# SEO Strategy & Optimisation Plan: "Care During Your Stay" Page (restwellretreats.co.uk)

> **Language update 2026-08-13:** Owner approved **respite** and **wheelchair friendly** in public copy. The only banned accessibility claim is **fully accessible**. Restwell is still not a care home or respite centre. Older “keep respite off-page” advice below is superseded.

## TL;DR
- Target this page at the lower-competition, higher-intent middle of the market — phrases like "accessible holiday with care," "self-catering with carer support," "CQC-regulated care on holiday," "bring your own carer," and **respite break / respite holiday with optional care** — not head terms such as "disabled holidays UK" or generic "respite holidays" owned by national directories (DisabledHolidays.com/Hays Travel, Limitless Travel, Revitalise, Carers Trust) that a single-property site cannot outrank as a primary.
- Use **respite** where carers and funders search it. Keep the honest split on the page: Restwell is a private adapted bungalow; Continuity of Care Services is the optional CQC-regulated provider; this is not a registered respite centre. "Short break with care support" remains a useful synonym, not a substitute that hides the word.
- **Wheelchair friendly** is allowed. Pair it with millimetres and kit. Never write **fully accessible**.
- Highest-impact actions: a keyword-aligned title/H1/slug built on "care during your stay" + accessible/respite holiday; Service + FAQPage schema with the property marked up as VacationRental/LodgingBusiness (never MedicalBusiness); and a tight internal-linking hub connecting this page to Accessibility, Funding & Support, How It Works, Who It's For and FAQ.

## Key Findings

### 1. The three audiences search in three different vocabularies
Research across competitor sites, charity/council directories and care-provider pages shows three distinct search languages this page must bridge:

- **Disabled self-bookers** use accommodation-first, feature-led phrases: "wheelchair accessible holiday cottage," "accessible self catering," "disabled holidays with hoists," "accessible cottage with wet room," "accessible bungalow by the sea," increasingly with a region attached ("accessible cottage Kent").
- **Unpaid carers / family bookers** use the care-and-charity sector's vocabulary, which is saturated with "respite": "respite holidays," "holidays for carers," "respite care holidays," "short breaks for carers," "carer supported holidays," "assisted holidays." Restwell may use this language on-page (2026-08-13). Still distinguish a **respite break in a private bungalow** from a **respite centre / care-home placement**.
- **Healthcare professionals (OTs, case managers, CHC teams, deputies)** search functionally: "accessible accommodation with care package," "CQC regulated care holiday," "supported short break complex needs," "care during a holiday," plus funding-linked terms ("direct payment holiday," "personal health budget short break"). Serious-injury case managers and solicitors (Stewarts, Irwin Mitchell, Boyes Turner) actively source funded holidays/short breaks for clients as part of rehabilitation planning — a warm, high-value referral channel almost no self-catering competitor addresses.

### 2. Use “respite” on-page; do not become a respite centre
"Respite" is the dominant term in the carer/charity ecosystem. Scope, Sense and Carers Trust all frame the category around it. Competitors lean in: DisabledHolidays.com runs "Respite Holidays" pages; Limitless Travel advertises optional respite care packages; Revitalise was built around respite grants.

The sector also uses "short breaks" (especially in children's services and some public-body language). Restwell can use **both**: respite where searchers and funders use it; short break as a synonym. Do not hide the word.

What we still will not do: describe Restwell as a **respite centre**, **care home**, or **nursing home**. The page must keep Restwell = accommodation, Continuity = optional CQC care.

Head terms such as generic "respite holidays UK" remain directory-owned. Do not make that the Care page primary. Use the winnable middle (care + bungalow + optional CQC / bring your own carer / respite break with published kit).

### 3. Competition is bifurcated — win the middle, not the head
- **Head terms are directory-owned.** "disabled holidays uk," "wheelchair accessible holidays," "accessible holiday cottages," "respite holidays," and "holidays for carers" are dominated by DisabledHolidays.com, Limitless Travel, Sykes, National Trust, Revitalise and Carers Trust. A single-property WordPress site will not reach page one for these and should not build the page around them.
- **The winnable middle** is the intersection of care + accommodation + specificity, where the big directories are thin and the real competitors are other owner-operators: "accessible holiday with optional care," "self catering with carer support," "CQC regulated care on holiday," "bring your own carer holiday," "accessible cottage with hoist Kent." Direct comparators are small independents (Lower Laurel Farm, Our Bench, Hawthorn Farm, Ellwood Cottages) plus one very close model — The Bond Hotel, Blackpool, whose care is delivered by a CQC-registered sister operation, Holidays With Care Ltd (registered at 120 Bond Street, Blackpool, FY4 1HG; the CQC describes it as "a personal care service supporting people with a range of disabilities and special needs whilst staying at the Bond Hotel in Blackpool"). Restwell's Restwell-plus-CCS structure is essentially the self-catering version of the Bond model — a strong signal that the model ranks and converts.

### 4. No reliable free search-volume figures exist for these niche terms
A dedicated volume investigation found that no free/public source publishes UK monthly-search-volume or keyword-difficulty numbers for these exact terms; concrete figures sit behind Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Keyword Planner logins. The one published UK dataset (Statista/Salience Search Marketing, September 2022) covers only generic holiday-cottage keywords ("holiday cottages" and pet/dog-friendly variants dominate) and does not break out accessible/disabled variants — itself confirming those variants are comparatively low-volume. Treat all volume/competition statements here as directional inferences from live SERP composition, to be validated in Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account, UK-filtered) before major investment.

### Schema.org structured data
Critical nuance: Restwell is accommodation, not a care setting. CQC regulates CCS, not Restwell. Under CQC's own guidance, where accommodation and personal care are provided separately and the guest can choose their care provider, the accommodation is not the regulated activity. Schema must preserve this distinction.

Recommended JSON-LD:
1. **VacationRental / LodgingBusiness** for the property entity (property page or site-wide), with `amenityFeature` (LocationFeatureSpecification) entries for the concrete equipment: ceiling track hoist, profiling bed, roll-in wet room, Geberit AquaClean WC, step-free access, off-street parking. This is the correct primary type for a single self-catering let and supports amenity/feature matching in search and AI results.
2. **Service** schema on THIS page for the optional care, with `provider` set to Continuity of Care Services as a separate `Organization`, not Restwell. Use a `serviceType` like "Personal care support during a holiday stay" and `areaServed` Whitstable/Kent. This cleanly models "Restwell = accommodation, CCS = the regulated care provider."
3. **FAQPage** schema for the page's FAQ block — the highest-value, lowest-risk markup, eligible for rich results. Mark up only Q&As visible on the page, with answers matching the on-page text.
4. **BreadcrumbList** (Home > Your stay > Care during your stay).
5. Represent **CCS as an Organization** with its CQC registration referenced via `sameAs` to the CQC location profile (cqc.org.uk/location/1-2624556588). Keep every care-quality/regulation claim attached to the CCS entity.
Do NOT apply MedicalBusiness, HealthAndBeautyBusiness or MedicalClinic to Restwell. If any care-oriented type is used at all, it belongs only to the CCS Organization object — and even then, Service + Organization is cleaner and safer.

### Content gaps competitors rank for that this page should cover
Drawn from Lower Laurel Farm, Our Bench, Hawthorn Farm, The Bond Hotel, DisabledHolidays.com, Helping Hands and Novus Care:
- **A concrete, plain-language care menu**: personal care, moving and handling, medication support, overnight/waking-night support, daily check-in. Competitors itemise this clearly; Restwell currently summarises it. The Bond Hotel's care arm even publishes exact rates — "Day Time (7am–midnight) £16 per hour per Carer; Waking Watch (10pm–8am) £160 per night per Carer; Sleeping Watch (10pm–8am) £110 per night plus room cost; Bank holidays are charged at double rate," plus a "Care Plan Fee: £100, this reduces to £50 if we have supported you within the past 6 months." Restwell need not publish CCS prices, but should explain that care is priced separately from accommodation and how the pricing conversation works.
- **"Bring your own carer/PA" logistics**: where the carer sleeps, parking for a carer's vehicle, and that there is no extra fee for bringing your own PA. Lower Laurel Farm and Our Bench both call out carer parking explicitly, and carers actively search this.
- **How care is arranged, with a timeline**: the care-plan conversation, lead times, and what CCS needs to know — reassurance aimed at professionals.
- **What "CQC-regulated" actually means and why it matters**: safety, accountability, and the contrast with unregulated introduction agencies (a distinction the care sector discusses openly), with the link to the CCS inspection profile.
- **A dedicated professional-referrer section** for OTs, case managers, CHC teams and deputies: immediate-needs assessments, funded rehabilitation breaks, and the documentation Restwell can provide for spend approval. No small self-catering competitor does this well; it is clear whitespace and matches the serious-injury/case-manager referral channel.
- **Care-specific funding signposting** (CHC, direct payments, personal health budgets, local authority short-break funding) linking through to the Funding & Support page.
- **Suitability by need/condition** (complex needs, progressive conditions, post-hospital, post-injury), written in Restwell's specific, non-emotive voice.

## Caveats
- No hard UK search-volume or keyword-difficulty numbers were available from free sources for these niche terms. All volume/competition statements are directional inferences from live SERP composition and sector data, and should be validated in Google Keyword Planner or a paid tool before major spend.
- "Respite" reflects genuine user search behaviour and **may be used on-page** (owner, 2026-08-13). The remaining trade-off is not hiding the word: do not position Restwell as a registered respite centre, and do not chase directory-owned head terms such as generic "respite holidays UK" as this page's primary.
- Schema must keep the accommodation/care distinction crisp. Labelling Restwell as a MedicalBusiness (rather than confining any care-type markup to the CCS Organization) risks both compliance/trust issues and inaccurate search classification.
- FAQ schema should mark up only Q&As genuinely visible on the page, with answers matching on-page text, or it may be ignored.
- Competitor pricing and equipment details cited here (e.g. Bond Hotel rates, Holidays With Care Ltd registration) can change over time; verify before referencing them publicly.
