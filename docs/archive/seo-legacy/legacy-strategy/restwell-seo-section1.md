## 1c. Semantic / NLP Keyword Clusters

### Cluster 1: "Accessible Coastal Holiday" (Homepage + Property Page)

**Primary page:** Homepage
**Supporting page:** The Property

| Role | Terms |
|------|-------|
| Primary keyword | accessible holiday cottage Kent coast |
| LSI / supporting | seaside, self-catering, coastal break, beach holiday, Kent shore, pebble beach, sea air, holiday let, short break, getaway |
| Entity terms | Whitstable, Kent, North Kent coast, English Channel, Canterbury |
| Equipment signals | level access, wet room, wide doorways, adapted kitchen, single storey |

**Content approach:** Homepage copy leads with the coastal holiday experience — sea, Whitstable charm, food, relaxation — and weaves accessibility features as built-in quality rather than selling points. The cluster positions Restwell as a holiday destination that happens to be fully accessible, not an accessible property that happens to be near the sea.

## Key Strategic Notes

### Where Restwell Can Realistically Rank

1. **Whitstable-specific accessible terms** — near-zero competition. Restwell should own these within 3–6 months of launch with basic on-page SEO and a Google Business Profile.

2. **"Accessible holiday cottage Kent"** — moderate competition. Aggregators (SimplyOwners, holidaycottages.co.uk, Bramley & Teal) currently dominate. Restwell can appear on page 1 with strong schema, GBP signals, and internal linking. Timeline: 6–12 months.

3. **Equipment-specific long-tail** ("holiday with hoist Kent", "holiday cottage wet room Kent") — low competition, very high intent. These are decision-stage searches. Restwell can rank for these quickly if the Accessibility Features page is comprehensive.

4. **Commissioner/professional terms** ("respite break disabled adult Kent", "CHC funded holiday placement") — almost no competition. Most properties don't use this language. A well-written "Who It's For" page targeting professionals could rank within weeks.

5. **"Revitalise alternatives"** — emerging search term following the 2024 closure. Zero competition from other individual properties. Blog content opportunity with high topical relevance.

### Where Restwell Will Struggle (and Shouldn't Waste Effort)

1. **National "disabled holidays UK" terms** — DisabledHolidays.com, Tourism for All, National Trust, and large aggregators dominate. Don't target these as primary keywords. Use them in blog content for awareness.

2. **"Accessible Airbnb" or "accessible self-catering UK"** — too broad, too much aggregator competition. Focus on Kent/coastal/Whitstable modifiers.

3. **Generic "Whitstable holiday cottage"** without the accessibility modifier — massive competition from Sykes, Keepers, Bloom Stays, Original Cottages. Restwell's niche is the differentiator; don't dilute it by competing on generic holiday let terms.
