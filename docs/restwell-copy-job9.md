**🏡 The house**

- Single-storey bungalow, step-free throughout, level threshold, no internal steps, no carpet lips
- Front door 965mm clear, internal doorways 926mm clear, firm level resin-paved route from driveway to door
- Open-plan living room (sofas, riser/recliner chair, dining table, feature fireplace, wall-mounted TV) leading into a conservatory
- Conservatory: double sofa bed, laundry facilities, garden views, threshold ramps onto the patio
- Kitchen: lowered/wheel-under worksurface, side-opening or sliding oven door
- Central/underfloor heating, free Wi-Fi throughout, welcome hamper

**🛏 Sleeping (sleeps 5, two bedrooms)**

- Accessible bedroom: two profiling beds, full-room-coverage ceiling track hoist, bedside aids, wall-mounted TV
- Mobile hoist and Sara Stedy stand aid usable in the bedroom and throughout the bungalow
- Second bedroom: double bed, wall-mounted TV, plus the conservatory sofa bed

**🚿 Wet room (installed by Care Spaces)**

- Roll-in, step-free shower with no lip and a floor-level drain
- Fixed and drop-down grab rails at the WC, washbasin and shower
- Geberit AquaClean wash/dry WC with remote and touch control
- Height-adjustable wheel-under washbasin that swings aside
- Shower/commode chair, tilt-in-space shower chair, perching stool
- Red emergency pull-cord alarm

**🌳 Outside**

- Enclosed, level-access garden with lawn, patio with outdoor dining and BBQ, dog-friendly and assistance dogs welcome (water bowls and toileting area)
- Hard-standing private driveway for two cars (adapted vehicles welcome, doubles as a step-free drop-off at the door), on-street parking usually nearby

**🚆 Getting there**

- Whitstable rail station about 2.6 miles / 9 min drive. Step-free to both platforms via separate street-level entrances (Old Bridge Road for Platform 1 to London, Railway Avenue for Platform 2 to Margate and Ramsgate). No step-free link between platforms, as the footbridge has stairs, so use the entrance for your direction.
- Nearest bus stop The Plough, about 0.2 miles / 4 min walk, routes 400, 903, 904, 905, 906
- Tankerton Beach a few minutes' walk with accessible promenade, Whitstable town and harbour about 20 min walk, Canterbury, Faversham and Herne Bay within easy drive
- Changing Places toilet at Whitstable Harbour (Harbour Road, RADAR key)

**🤝 Care and formats**

- Care arranged locally through Continuity Care Services, from companionship and domiciliary care to complex and 24/7 support
- Access information available in Easy Read, large print and audio on request
- Contact: [hello@restwellretreats.co.uk](mailto:hello@restwellretreats.co.uk), 01622 809881, Russell Drive, Whitstable CT5 2RQ

## 💷 Funding & Support (`template-resources.php`)

**H1:** How to fund an accessible holiday in the UK

**Intro:** There are several ways people pay for a break like this. The most common are direct payments, a personal budget under the Care Act, and NHS Continuing Healthcare. The right route depends on your circumstances, so treat this as a starting point and check the detail with your local authority or care team.

**Body (plain explainer):**

- Direct payments: money from your local authority that you arrange yourself, which can sometimes cover a respite stay. Confirm in WP: how Restwell supports direct-payment bookings.
- Personal budget (Care Act): a planned amount for your care and support that may include short breaks. Speak to your social worker about what it can cover.
- NHS Continuing Healthcare: funding for some people with significant health needs. Your care team can tell you if it applies.
- Carer's support: your local carers service may help with the cost of a break. Confirm in WP: local links.

**Meta title:** Funding an Accessible Respite Holiday | Restwell

**Meta description:** How families fund an accessible respite break: direct payments, personal budgets under the Care Act, and NHS Continuing Healthcare. A plain-English guide.

**FAQ:**

- **Q:** Can I use a direct payment to stay at Restwell? **A:** Sometimes, depending on your care plan. Ask your local authority, and we can provide what you need for the request. Confirm in WP: documents you provide.
- **Q:** Will my personal budget cover a short break? **A:** It can, if short breaks are part of your plan. Your social worker can confirm.
- **Q:** Can NHS Continuing Healthcare fund a holiday? **A:** CHC can sometimes fund your assessed care needs while you're away, though not usually the accommodation itself. Your care team can confirm, and our CHC guide explains the detail.
- **Q:** Can carers get help paying for a break? **A:** Sometimes. A carer's assessment can unlock support for short breaks, and your local carers service may help with costs. Confirm in WP: local links.
